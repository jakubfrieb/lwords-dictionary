## Word lists and level data

The level files (`levels_en_*.json`, `levels_cs_*.json`) and the derived word lists
(`wordlists/en/words.txt`, `wordlists/cs/words.txt`) are an **adaptation** of the sources
below: words were selected, filtered (proper nouns, abbreviations, offensive words removed),
ranked by frequency and combined into puzzles. The level data and the derived word lists are
licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

- **wordfreq 3.1.1** by Robyn Speer et al. – data CC BY-SA 4.0, code Apache 2.0,
  https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq (doi:10.5281/zenodo.7199437). Used for frequency ranking
  (English and Czech).
  wordfreq contains data from Google Books Ngrams (http://books.google.com/ngrams),
  Wikipedia (http://www.wikipedia.org), the Leeds Internet Corpus
  (http://corpus.leeds.ac.uk/list.html), ParaCrawl (https://paracrawl.eu), OPUS OpenSubtitles
  2018 (http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles.php), whose data originates from the OpenSubtitles
  project (http://www.opensubtitles.org/), and from the **SUBTLEX** word lists (SUBTLEX-US, -UK,
  -CH, -DE, -NL) created by **Marc Brysbaert et al.** – SUBTLEX is freely available data,
  http://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies.
- **Universal Dependencies 2.18** (https://universaldependencies.org/), CC BY-SA 4.0 – Czech
  lemmas and parts of speech:
  - UD_Czech-CAC – Barbora Hladká, Daniel Zeman
  - UD_Czech-CLTT – Barbora Hladká, Daniel Zeman, Martin Popel
  - UD_Czech-PUD – Václava Kettnerová, Jan Hajič jr., Silvie Cinková, Zdeňka Urešová,
    Milan Straka, Jan Hajič, Jaroslava Hlaváčová, Daniel Zeman
- **12dicts** (2of12id, 3esl, abbr; alt12dicts 2020.12.07) by Alan Beale – public domain,
  http://wordlist.aspell.net/12dicts/ (English headwords and word filtering).
- **ENABLE** (enable1) word list – public domain (English spell check).
